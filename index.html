Platform Architecture & Guide

How to Use Voxora

  1. Click the glowing Start Voice Call button and grant microphone permission when prompted.
  2. Speak naturally. When you stop speaking, Voxora processes your voice and replies immediately.
  3. You can interrupt Voxora anytime by speaking — the audio will yield to you.
  4. Press Spacebar anytime to mute/unmute your microphone.
  5. Open the Voice Studio to test all 8 natural voices or switch between 4 visualizer modes.
